HUMANITY IN ACTION
WHO WE ARE

Compassion in action.
Justice for all.

Karnataka Human Rights Public Service Committee is focused on humanitarian assistance, social welfare and advocacy support. Our approach is strictly non-political and centered on human dignity.

Through community outreach programs and support events, we aim to stand beside individuals and families who need a helping hand.
